CRITICAL BUG FIXED
================================================================================

BEFORE (WRONG):
    video_start_system_time = labjack_start_time + (startup_delay_ms / 1000.0)
    
    Result: NEGATIVE LATENCY (-1578ms in test case)
    Cause:  Ground truth events calculated too late in system time

AFTER (CORRECT):
    video_start_system_time = labjack_start_time
    
    Result: POSITIVE LATENCY (+154ms in test case)
    Reason: Video timeline t=0 aligns with labjack_start_time

================================================================================
WHY THIS IS CORRECT
================================================================================

1. labjack_start_time = Unix epoch timestamp (absolute system time)
2. startup_delay_ms = buffering duration (NOT a timestamp offset)
3. Video timeline t=0 corresponds to labjack_start_time
4. Ground truth times are measured from video t=0
5. Therefore: gt_system_time = labjack_start_time + gt_video_time
